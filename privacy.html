Chalk Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: Chalk keeps your training log in your own account. It sends a summary of your training to an AI provider only when you use the online Coach. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools, and your data is never sold. You can export or delete everything from the app.

Who runs Chalk

Chalk is an iPhone app for CrossFit and strength training, built and run by one developer as a personal project. Questions about this policy or your data: lior87@gmail.com.

What Chalk stores

  • Your account: the email address and account id from Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • What you enter: workouts and scores, a daily check-in (energy, soreness, sleep), lifts and records, benchmarks, goals, skills, body weight and body fat, recovery routines, supplements, equipment, preferences, and injuries with any notes you add about them (which can include what a doctor or physio told you).
  • Coach answers: each answer, a short summary of what was asked, the model used and how many tokens it took, so you can see your usage and the Coach can avoid repeating itself. Things you ask the Coach to remember are stored too, and you can change or delete them in Account.
  • An AI key, if you add your own: stored encrypted, and used only to answer your own requests.

Your full chat history stays on your iPhone and is not uploaded.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Photos: a photo you scan to fill in a workout is read on the iPhone. The photo is not uploaded, only the text you choose to save.
  • The on-device Coach: the default Coach runs on Apple's on-device model. Nothing leaves your iPhone to answer.
  • Gym login: if you connect Arbox, your Arbox login is kept in the iPhone's Keychain, and Chalk talks to Arbox directly from your iPhone.

Apple Health

With your permission, Chalk reads your body weight, the workouts your Apple Watch recorded, and last night's sleep and your resting heart rate. Weight and workouts are shown next to what you log. Body weight you import is saved to your account, and so are each day's hours of sleep and resting heart rate, with the check-in you can fill in on Today (energy and soreness), so the Coach can plan around how you feel. Chalk never writes to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone for that purpose.

The online Coach

If you switch the Coach to online and ask it something, Chalk sends your question and a summary of your training to Anthropic (Claude), using Chalk's key or your own Claude key. The summary covers recent workouts, lifts, goals, injuries, preferences and your recent check-ins (with sleep and resting heart rate if you allow them from Apple Health), and your gym's coming classes if you connected Arbox.

Anthropic handles that data under its API terms and privacy policy. Nothing the Coach suggests becomes part of your log unless you save it.

Friends

If you use Friends, you choose a name and get a friend code. Only people you give your code to can add you, and there is no search or list of people. A friend, and anyone you have a pending request with, sees your name. A friend also sees the workouts you send them, with your note and your score if you include it. Nobody sees the rest of your log, your injuries or your records. When a friend opens a workout you sent, Chalk works out their loads from their own records on their side. Removing a friend stops new workouts, and deleting your account removes your name, your friendships and every workout you sent or received.

Where your data is kept

Your account and log are stored with Supabase, in its Seoul (South Korea) region. Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules make sure each account can read only its own rows.

What Chalk does not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools. Apple may share crash reports with the developer if you allow it in your iPhone's settings.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone other than the services named above and the friends you choose, as described under Friends.

Your choices

  • Export: Account → Export gives you everything Chalk holds about you as a file.
  • Delete: Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything in it from the server, and removes the Arbox login from your iPhone. You can also email the address above to ask for this.
  • Stop sending data to an AI provider: set the Coach to "On this iPhone" in Account.
  • Apple Health: you can turn off Chalk's access at any time in the Health app.

Children

Chalk is not meant for children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Anything that changes what is shared will also be announced in the app.
